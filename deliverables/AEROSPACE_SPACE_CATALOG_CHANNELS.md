## 5. “AVOX-style” aviation catalogs — clarification

| Item | Finding |
|------|---------|
| **Note** | **AVOX** historically refers to aviation oxygen / cabin safety product lines (and related brands), **not** a general open parts-listing marketplace analogous to PartsBase/ILS. Do **not** treat AVOX as a self-serve catalog apply path for LC-Flow. |
| **Adjacent primes / distribution** | Broad aerospace distribution (e.g. **Boeing Distribution Services**, formerly Aviall) and prime supplier networks are **invitation / quality-system / category-manager** paths — closed BD, not McMaster-style public apply. |

Competitive, long cycle; quality expectations typically include accredited QMS (AS9100 manufacturer / AS9120 distributor common for aerospace parts). |

**Official links**

- Boeing — Becoming a Supplier: https://www.boeingsuppliers.com/become  
- ESLC invitation request: email `boeingassessment@boeing.com` with contact name/title/email/phone, company name/address, business size (per Boeing page instructions)  
- Exostar (aerospace trading exchange; Boeing encourages suppliers): https://www.exostar.com
