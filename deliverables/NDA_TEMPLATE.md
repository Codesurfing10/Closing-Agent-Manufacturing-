## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to exchange confidential information solely for the purpose of evaluating a
potential commercial relationship regarding the **LC-Flow Valve and Distribution Adaptor**
technology and related products (SKUs including LCP061000, LCSS61000, LCA061000), including
technical specifications, materials, process data, pricing, drawings, CAD, samples, and
application engineering (the "**Purpose**").

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

"**Confidential Information**" means non-public information disclosed by either Party in any form
(written, oral, electronic, or sample) that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person
would understand to be confidential, including without limitation: product designs, drawings,
CAD files, material formulations, manufacturing processes (including additive manufacturing
methods), performance data, pricing, quotes, customer lists, roadmaps, and business terms.

## 3. Obligations

Each Receiving Party shall: (a) use Confidential Information only for the Purpose; (b) protect it
with at least reasonable care; (c) not disclose it to third parties except to employees/advisors
with a need to know who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective; and
(d) not reverse engineer samples except as expressly permitted in writing.

## 4. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes public through no
fault of the Receiving Party; (b) was already known without confidentiality duty; (c) is
independently developed without use of the other Party's Confidential Information; (d) is
rightfully received from a third party without confidentiality duty; or (e) is required to be
disclosed by law or court order (with prompt notice to the Disclosing Party to the extent legally
permitted).

## 5. Term

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for **three (3) years**, unless
terminated earlier by mutual written consent. Confidentiality obligations survive for
**three (3) years** after termination or disclosure (whichever is later) for trade secrets, for so
long as they remain trade secrets under applicable law.

## 6. No License / No Obligation to Deal

Nothing herein grants a license under any IP, or obligates either Party to proceed with a
transaction, purchase, or design-in.

## 7. Return / Destruction

Upon written request, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy Confidential
Information (except one archival copy retained solely for legal/compliance purposes).

## 8. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of **Pennsylvania**, USA,
without regard to conflict-of-law principles, **unless counsel substitutes another jurisdiction**.
